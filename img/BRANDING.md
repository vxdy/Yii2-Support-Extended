# Yii2 Insight logo variants

- `yii2-insight-marketplace.svg`: 40 x 40 Marketplace icon with transparent padding,
  based on the Yii mark from `resources/META-INF/pluginIcon.svg`.
- `yii2-insight-github.png`: horizontal Yii mark and Yii2 Insight wordmark for the README.

Yii2 Insight is published and maintained by by_scrimm. The Yii mark is inherited
framework artwork; artwork-rights review remains a release step before Marketplace use.

Format reference: https://plugins.jetbrains.com/docs/intellij/plugin-icon-file.html
